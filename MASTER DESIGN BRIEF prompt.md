PART 4 — TYPOGRAPHY RULES  
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\- \*\*Main headlines: 2-3 lines max. NEVER 4+.\*\* Force copy short enough to GUARANTEE this on desktop.  
\- \*\*Headlines 2-3x larger than sub-headlines.\*\*  
\- \*\*Headline weight: medium or semi-bold.\*\* Heavier only if niche is brutalist by design OR font IS the brand identity.  
\- \*\*Italic accent on ONE word\*\* allowed with serif — italicize nouns/identity words, not verbs.  
\- \*\*Eyebrow text content:\*\* NEVER generic labels ("THE PROBLEM", "OUR FEATURES"). Write emotional, page-specific micro-copy.  
\- \*\*Eyebrow styling:\*\* all caps OR sentence case, 12-14px, letter-spaced 0.05-0.1em, brand accent color.  
\- \*\*Sub-headline:\*\* 1-2 sentences max, 30-50% of headline size, regular/light weight.

\#\#\# NO LETTER CLIPPING (critical)  
\- \*\*Line-height must accommodate ascenders AND descenders.\*\* Letters like j, p, q, g, y must show their full descender. Letters like b, d, f, h, k, l, t must show their full ascender. NEVER let line-height (or container height) crop any character.  
\- \*\*When using gradient text, text-clip, or any masked text effect:\*\*  
  \- Add extra padding (10-15% of font-size) to the top and bottom of the text container  
  \- Set \`line-height\` to at least 1.15-1.25 even on huge display type  
  \- Verify visually that no character bottom (especially \`g\`, \`j\`, \`p\`, \`q\`, \`y\`) is cut off  
  \- This is critical because gradient/clip masks often crop the visible area to the type metrics  
\- \*\*Still maintain perfect line spacing\*\* when fixing this — don't add padding asymmetrically; balance top and bottom.

\- \*\*Approved two-font pairings:\*\* Söhne \+ Tiempos / Aeonik \+ Editorial New / Helvetica Now \+ GT Sectra / PP Neue Montreal \+ Canela / Inter Display \+ Recoleta.  
\- \*\*2-color headline:\*\* both from brand palette, both WCAG AA, both readable.  
\- \*\*Body copy:\*\* 16-18px desktop, 1.5-1.7 line-height, 65-75 char max line.

\*\*Headline copy patterns:\*\* Outcome promise / Mechanism reveal / Authority claim / Curiosity hook / Direct command / Editorial statement / Product declarative

\#\#\# BENTO GRID RULES (critical — bento is mandatory on most pages)

A bento grid is a tessellated layout where cards of varying sizes fit together perfectly with NO gaps, NO empty cells, NO orphaned space.

\- \*\*Use a consistent underlying grid\*\* (6-col or 12-col). Every card is sized in whole units of that grid.  
\- \*\*Every cell of the grid must be filled.\*\* No empty space. No L-shaped holes. No awkward gaps where a card "would have gone."  
\- \*\*If you have 5, 7, or odd-numbered cards\*\* that don't fit neatly into a rectangle, RESIZE cards (make one larger to fill 2 cells, or merge two small cards into one wide card) until every cell is occupied.  
\- \*\*Common bento layouts that always tessellate:\*\*  
  \- 4 items → 2x2 equal  
  \- 5 items → 1 hero (spans 2x2) \+ 4 small cards around it (2 above, 2 below OR 2 right, 2 below)  
  \- 6 items → 1 hero (2x2) \+ 4 small in a 2x2 \+ 1 wide bottom (2 cols wide)  
  \- 7 items → 1 hero (2x2) \+ 6 small in 2 rows of 3 OR 1 hero \+ 1 wide \+ 5 small arranged to fill  
  \- 8 items → 2x4 grid OR 1 hero \+ 7 fills via mixed sizing  
\- \*\*Before delivering the bento, mentally diff the grid.\*\* If you see any visible gap, resize cards until the grid is solid.  
\- \*\*Card heights must align\*\* when they share a row. No "shorter card with empty space below it."

\#\#\# Variation rule

\- \*\*Icon library defaults\*\* (when generating): Phosphor, Lucide, Tabler, Iconoir — all modern, premium-feeling. Avoid Font Awesome free tier and stock emoji.  
\- \*\*If a section truly needs sequential markers\*\* (a 3-step process where order matters), use icons WITH small step indicators ("Step 1 of 3" in micro-copy), not big \`\[01\]\` brackets.
